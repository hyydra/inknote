InkNote is a handwriting-first notebook. Open the app to start writing on the launcher canvas, or browse and edit your saved notes.

Use a pencil, pen, highlighter or eraser. Add typed text and checklists, and choose plain, lined, grid or dotted paper. Pin a note preview to a resizable Android home screen widget. Tap the widget to open the writing canvas.

Notes are stored locally. On Android, you can manually sync with a folder selected through the system picker.
